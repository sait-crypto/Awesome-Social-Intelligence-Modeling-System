Definition

What is SIM?

AI has studied society for years, yet the field remains fragmented. Research on social intelligence appear in different areas such as sentiment analysis, misinformation detection, user modeling, content generation, multi-agent systems, and social simulation. Because these studies use different task names and keywords, it is difficult to see how they connect or to form a clear view of the field as a whole.

Our Social Intelligence Modeling (SIM) brings these fragments together in one framework. By organizing nearly 1,000 papers across 20+ tasks, we show how AI models social content, people, relations, and social evolution. SIM structures the field into four connected levels: Perception, Understanding, Generation, and Simulation, providing a unified view of how AI engages with social systems.

Perception

Recognize social signals and states in content, users, and multimodal interactions.

Understanding

Model social structure, discourse, networks, communities, and behavioral patterns.

Generation

Produce socially situated content, dialogue, counter-speech, and interventions.

Simulation

Reconstruct dynamic social processes and explore plausible collective futures.
