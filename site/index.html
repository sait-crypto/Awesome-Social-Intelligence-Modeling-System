Definition

What is Social Intelligence Modeling?

Social Intelligence Modeling (SIM) develops computational models that represent and reason about social signals, actors, relations, interactions, and evolving collective processes. Its scope runs from detecting a single social cue to simulating a society.

Perception

Recognize social signals and states in content, users, and multimodal interactions.

Understanding

Model social structure, discourse, networks, communities, and behavioral patterns.

Generation

Produce socially situated content, dialogue, counter-speech, and interventions.

Simulation

Reconstruct dynamic social processes and explore plausible collective futures.
